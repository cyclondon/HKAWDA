News & events

Factory tours, council meetings and gatherings that keep members up to date.

  1. 2025-11-29 · SAT

    Zhongshan aluminium window & glass factory tour

    Cross-border coaches from Mong Kok and Tuen Mun took members to Zhongshan to visit founding member LE-RELAX's Zhongshan production base (Zhen Wei aluminium window factory), Zhen Sheng glass factory and Xinyi Glass, with a lunch gathering. The tour helped members see the latest trends and production techniques and share experience with industry professionals.

  2. 2023-12-09

    Zhongshan factory visit

    Nearly fifty council members and members toured founding member LE-RELAX's Zhongshan factory, including its aluminium window lines and glass processing, followed by lunch with suppliers.

  3. 2023-10-16

    First council meeting of 2023

    The first council gathering since the pandemic. Members discussed mainland factory tours, a Class III minor works course with IVE, and asking government to restore short courses for Class II minor works.

  4. 2015-11-30

    Founding ceremony and 2015 members' dinner

    Close to a hundred guests attended. Founding President Lee Siu-po said the Association would unite the trade to speak with one voice and serve the public better. Council Chairman Chan Yu-bing said the first priority was a member register so residents could find qualified professionals.
