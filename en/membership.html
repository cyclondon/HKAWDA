Join HKAWDA

We welcome everyone in the trade. Together we can speak to government for the industry and build its future. All memberships are lifetime.

CATEGORYFORFEE (HKD)

HOW TO APPLY

How to apply

  1. Download and complete the form

    Fill in company details and category, then sign and stamp.

  2. Send it with documents

    Email the form with a copy of your Business Registration and a business card to info@hkawda.org, or WhatsApp 6915 6828.

  3. Pay the fee

    Pay by cash, cheque or bank transfer; details are on the form. Cheques payable to Hong Kong Aluminium Windows & Doors Association Company Limited.

  4. Approval and welcome pack

    Once approved, you receive your card, certificate and sticker, and your firm is added to the directory.
