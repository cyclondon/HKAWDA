Industry courses

We partner with the Engineering Discipline of the Hong Kong Institute of Vocational Education (IVE) to offer courses for the window and door trade.

IVE ENGINEERING

Professional Development Course on Windows Installation and Maintenance

Covers window installation, maintenance and safety requirements.

MBIS / MWIS

MBIS & MWIS top-up courses

For Level 2 Representatives, TCP-T1 and Authorised Signatories.

GLASS & FAÇADE

Testing and Inspection of Glass and Façade Works

Testing standards and inspection methods for glass and façade works.

CLASS III MINOR WORKS

Class III minor works course

Arranged with IVE and open to all members (fee-paying).

ADVOCACY

Training for the next generation

We are asking government to bring back short courses for Class II minor works, so more young people can enter the trade, and to recognise window fitters' qualifications.

ENROL

Course enquiries

Dates and fees are announced to members. To register interest, contact Member Liaison.
