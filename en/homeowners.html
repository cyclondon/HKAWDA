Replacing aluminium windows in Hong Kong: a homeowner guide

What to check before, during and after replacing your windows, on one page.

THE RULES

Window replacement is "minor works"

Under the Buildings Department's Minor Works Control System, repairing or replacing a window is usually a Class I, II or III minor works item, depending on height above ground, frame size and whether the main frame is involved. Works no more than 3.5 m above ground, for example, generally fall under Class III item 3.6.

The work must be carried out by a contractor registered with the Buildings Department for the relevant class and type, with the required documents submitted to the Department. Before signing, ask the contractor which class and item applies and who will make the submission.

Buildings Department: minor works related to windows ↗
MWIS

Served a window inspection notice?

The Buildings Department's Mandatory Window Inspection Scheme covers private buildings aged ten years or more. Owners served a statutory notice must appoint a qualified person to inspect their windows, with any repair or replacement carried out by a registered contractor.

Many members are familiar with the inspection and repair process; ask about it when you request a quotation.

QUOTATIONS

What a written quotation should include

When comparing quotes, compare materials and responsibilities, not only price.

01 FRAME

Profile series and wall thickness

e.g. 50 or 70 series, wall thickness and finish (powder-coated or anodised).

02 GLASS

Glass type and thickness

Tempered, laminated or insulated glazing, and thickness in mm.

03 HARDWARE

Hinges, latches and rivets

Material and brand of stainless-steel friction stays, latches and rivets.

04 SCOPE

Removal, sealing and clean-up

Whether it covers removing the old window, sealing, debris disposal and making good.

05 COMPLIANCE

Minor works submission

Class and item, and the name and registration number of the registered contractor making the submission.

06 WARRANTY

Warranty and payment terms

Warranty period and scope, and stage payments rather than full payment upfront.

HANDOVER

Handover checklist

  1. Open and close every sash several times; it should run smoothly and seal when latched.

  2. Check the sealant around the frame is continuous with no gaps or cracks.

  3. Confirm glass, hardware and profiles match the quotation.

  4. Collect the warranty and copies of the minor works submission documents.

  5. Make sure the site is cleaned and debris removed.

MAINTENANCE

Everyday window safety

  1. Regularly check hinges and rivets for rust, looseness or missing parts.

  2. Latches should lock firmly; close and latch every window before a typhoon.

  3. Don't hang heavy items outside windows or force the sashes.

  4. If a sash sags, wobbles or won't close, stop using it and have a member firm inspect it promptly.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the Association carry out work itself?

No. HKAWDA is a trade association; member firms quote for and carry out the work directly. Use the member directory to search by district.

How do I confirm a firm is a member?

Members usually display the HKAWDA sticker and hold a numbered membership card. Search the company name in the directory, or email info@hkawda.org.

How long does a window replacement take?

It depends on the number and size of windows and fabrication lead time. Ask the firm for measuring, ordering and installation timings and put them in the contract.

Can I raise a concern about a member's work?

Yes. Please raise it with the firm first; if unresolved, email us and we will follow up. The Association is not an arbitrator; for contract disputes, consider the Consumer Council or legal advice.
